Continuing Education & Professional Development

ITECCS Continuing Education supports lifelong learning for professionals working in applied mathematics, engineering simulation, artificial intelligence, scientific computing, and related technical fields. Programs are designed to fit the schedules of working professionals and to deliver measurable capability in a compact format.

Professional certificates and Continuing Professional Education (CPE) credits designed to strengthen career pathways and meet industry-recognized standards.

Flexible formats including online, hybrid, and self-paced modules so learning fits around work, research, and family commitments.

Stackable micro-credentials that combine into larger certificates, letting learners progress module by module without enrolling in a full program upfront.

Whether your goal is a focused skill update, a recognized credential, or a long-term capability build-out, ITECCS Continuing Education provides a pathway that is rigorous, practical, and aligned with modern computational and engineering practice.

Our Continuing Education offerings span the full range of computational and engineering disciplines, with each pathway focused on a clearly defined set of professional outcomes.

CPE-eligible coursework for licensed engineers and technical professionals.

Corporate & Team Training

Customized programs for engineering and research teams at the organizational level.

  • Hands-on workshops delivered on-site or online

Micro-Credentials & Stackable Pathways
