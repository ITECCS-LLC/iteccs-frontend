Institute of Technology, Engineering, Computing, and Computational Sciences

ITECCS is a premier research and education institute advancing applied mathematics, artificial intelligence, engineering, and scientific computing. We provide high-quality education and advanced professional training, and collaborate globally with universities, industry, and government partners to address complex scientific and technological challenges. Through rigorous mathematical modeling, high-performance computing, and intelligent computational methods, ITECCS empowers researchers, engineers, and institutions to translate scientific insight into deployable technologies and real-world impact. Our institute serves as a trusted platform for innovation, enabling organizations to strengthen their scientific capabilities, accelerate engineering development, and lead technological advancement in an increasingly computational world. ITECCS is an independent, online research and training institute operating remotely and serving clients across Wisconsin, the United States, and internationally. ITECCS operates as a private, non-degree research and training initiative and is not affiliated with any university.
