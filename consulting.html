Technical Advisory & Industry Collaboration

The ITECCS Research Center provides internationally recognized expertise at the intersection of applied mathematics, artificial intelligence, scientific computing, and advanced engineering simulation. Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about ITECCS consulting and industry collaboration.

ITECCS offers technical advisory in scientific computing, computational modeling, CFD consulting, finite element analysis (FEA) consulting, multiphysics simulation, high-performance computing (HPC), digital twin consulting, and scientific machine learning consulting.
ITECCS works with universities, research laboratories, industry R&D teams, engineering firms, startups, and government agencies on technical advisory, computational modeling, and research-to-deployment projects.
Multiphysics simulation consulting involves modeling systems where multiple physical processes interact, such as fluid flow, heat transfer, structural deformation, and electromagnetic effects. ITECCS develops and validates these models for engineering and scientific applications.
Companies typically seek CFD or FEA consulting when internal teams lack specialized expertise, when models need rigorous validation, when simulations must scale on HPC infrastructure, or when translating research methods into production workflows.
Digital twin consulting involves integrating physics-based models, real-time data, and AI-based inference to create predictive virtual replicas of physical systems. ITECCS helps design and validate digital twin architectures for engineering, energy, and industrial systems.
